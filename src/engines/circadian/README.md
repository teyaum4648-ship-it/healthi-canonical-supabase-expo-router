# Circadian Engine

Responsibilities:

- Estimate sleep need.
- Calculate nightly and 7-day sleep debt.
- Generate circadian energy curve.
- Identify current energy phase.
- Recommend training, walking, caffeine, and wind-down windows.

Use estimated language. Do not claim to measure cortisol without lab data.

Default windows:

- Alertness rise: wake + 30 to 60 min
- Peak 1: wake + 1.5 to 3 hours
- Dip: wake + 6 to 8 hours
- Peak 2: wake + 9 to 11 hours
- Wind-down: target bedtime - 90 min
