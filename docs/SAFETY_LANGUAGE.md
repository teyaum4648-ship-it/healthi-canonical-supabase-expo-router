# Safety and Language Guide

Healthi is a wellness guidance app. It is not a medical provider.

## Approved language

Use:

- estimate
- suggest
- may indicate
- flag
- screen
- guidance
- wellness
- general recommendation
- consider
- may be useful
- based on your recent pattern
- confidence is low/medium/high
- consult a qualified professional

## Avoid language

Do not use:

- diagnose
- treat
- cure
- prescribe
- clinical assessment
- medical decision
- guaranteed
- disease reversal
- detox disease claims
- adrenal fatigue diagnosis
- hormone diagnosis
- cortisol measured, unless actual lab data is present
- inflammatory as a definitive medical claim from a food scan

## Workout safety

If user reports pain:

Bad:

```txt
Push through it.
```

Good:

```txt
Healthi is removing this loaded pattern today because you reported pain. Use the corrective session instead and consider a qualified professional if pain persists or feels serious.
```

## Food safety

Allergies override everything.

Bad:

```txt
This food is clean, so you can try it anyway.
```

Good:

```txt
This item is blocked because it conflicts with your allergy settings.
```

## Medical red flags

If user reports any of these, stop normal guidance and recommend professional care:

- Chest pain.
- Fainting.
- Severe dizziness.
- Shortness of breath at rest.
- New neurological symptoms.
- Severe injury.
- Pregnancy-related medical concern.
- Eating disorder history with restrictive behavior.
- Severe depression or self-harm signals.

## Eating disorder and restriction safety

Avoid:

- Extreme calorie restriction.
- Moralizing food language.
- Shame about eating.
- Punishment workouts.
- Encouraging fasting when risk flags exist.

Use:

- nourishment.
- stable energy.
- recovery support.
- familiar foods.
- protein-forward.
- hydration.
- digestion-friendly.

## Readiness language

Bad:

```txt
Your body is broken today.
```

Good:

```txt
Your recovery capacity looks reduced today, so Healthi is lowering intensity and focusing on restoration.
```

## Ingredient language

Bad:

```txt
This ingredient is toxic.
```

Good:

```txt
This item contains ingredients some users prefer to limit. Your personal response history matters most, so Healthi will track how you respond if you choose to log it.
```
