# Founder Notes and Product Intent

These notes preserve the intent behind Healthi so future coding agents do not reduce the product into isolated features.

## The problem

Workout routines and many personal trainers do not consistently account for posture, flexibility, mobility, allostatic load, sleep, food, hydration, and actual performance response.

Food quality apps can say a product is generally clean or not, but they do not adapt to the user's personal response.

Recovery apps can track sleep or HRV, but they often do not connect that data to food quality, posture, exercise selection, grocery shopping, and daily lifestyle decisions.

## The product insight

Healthi should be the missing interpretation layer.

It should connect:

- Sleep affects training.
- Circadian rhythm affects when to train, eat, caffeinate, walk, and wind down.
- Training affects food needs.
- Food affects sleep, energy, digestion, mood, and training.
- Posture affects exercise selection.
- Mobility affects loading permission.
- Hydration affects energy and performance.
- Stress affects cravings, recovery, and readiness.
- Personal response beats generic advice.

## Training intent

The user believes in progressive overload, but not reckless progression.

A productive workout should show at least one small improvement when recovery is adequate. Example:

Previous session:

```txt
Bench Press: 225 x 5
DB Bench: 70s x 8
Smith Machine Incline: 185 x 10
```

Next session:

```txt
Maybe everything stays the same except DB Bench becomes 70s x 9.
```

That counts. It means the body recovered enough to adapt.

But if the user cannot progress, Healthi should ask why:

- Did they sleep poorly?
- Did they eat enough?
- Did they hydrate?
- Did caffeine overstimulate them?
- Are they sore?
- Are they stressed?
- Is posture or mobility blocking the pattern?
- Have they been training too hard?

Then Healthi changes the day accordingly.

## Recovery intent

If the user is overloaded, Healthi should not shame them or tell them to force it. It should replace the workout with a restorative plan:

- Walking to discharge caffeine jitters or stress.
- Dark-room stretching or outdoor sunlight depending on circadian need.
- Breathwork.
- Mobility.
- Cooking and nourishing if under-fueled.
- Wind-down and sleep protection if sleep-deprived.

## Food intent

Healthi should not only ask what the user ate. It should ask how the user responded.

Food logging should lead to personal intelligence:

- Which foods produce stable energy?
- Which foods produce bloating or fatigue?
- Which foods support training?
- Which foods hurt sleep?
- Which foods should be bought again?
- Which foods should be reduced?

## Shopping intent

The shopping assistant should be practical and contextual.

User says:

```txt
I'm going to Costco. Based on my meal plan, what should I get?
```

Healthi should use:

- Meal plan
- Personal food response
- Food quality rules
- Goal
- Budget
- Current readiness
- Digestion
- Training schedule

And output:

- Shopping list
- Recipes
- Avoid/limit section
- Meal prep plan
- Why each major item fits

## Product identity

Healthi should feel like a practitioner-style adaptive system, not a gym app or calorie app.

Tone:

- Calm
- Premium
- Specific
- Non-shaming
- Direct
- Explanatory
- Grounded in user data

## Most important product sentence

The user's goal gives direction. The body's current state gives permission.
