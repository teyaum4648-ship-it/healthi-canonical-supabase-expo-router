## Description draft

Bring your daily wellness habits together with Daywoven.

Log meals and water, understand your sleep rhythm, record movement, and build routines that fit your day. Daywoven brings your available information together to suggest a daily focus and explain the signals behind it.

- Keep a nutrition diary with food search, barcode lookup and manual entry.
- Track sleep times, hydration, workouts and everyday habits.
- Follow guided breathing practices and reflect on how your day felt.
- Review patterns and build a daily plan around recovery, nourishment, movement and life load.
- Choose whether to connect Apple Health for read-only sleep and steps, when supported by your device and granted permission.

Daywoven provides general wellness guidance, not diagnosis or treatment. Estimates may be incomplete or inaccurate. Food records cannot guarantee that a product is free of allergens; always check its label. Consult a qualified professional for medical concerns.
