Your Day Mode

Not every day is a day to push. daywoven tells you which one this is.

  • Restore

    Short night, heavy week. Recover first, and keep the rest of the day light.

  • Nourish

    Running on empty. Eat well and eat on time, and the rest follows.

  • Correct

    Something’s off rhythm. One small change brings the day back.

  • Move

    Steady and ready. A good day to train well, without forcing it.

  • Perform

    Everything lines up. Use it for the session or the work that matters.

Readiness

One number for today, and the reasons behind it.

Recovery, nourishment, movement and load, pulled into a single score each morning. It always shows how sure it is and what moved it, so you can decide for yourself.

Recovery + Rhythm

Know when your energy peaks, and when the dip is coming.

Your sleep window and your own rhythm map the day into focus windows and slow stretches. The afternoon slump stops being a surprise and becomes something you plan around.

Nourishment

See how a food scores before you eat it.

Search or scan almost anything. Every score comes with its reasons, and the allergens you care about are flagged straight from the label. No calorie guilt, just clearer choices.

Mind + Life Load

When the day gets heavy, a minute or two to reset.

Short breathing and reset practices, and a load check that makes sure a hard week isn’t treated like an easy one. The plan bends to the day you’re actually having.

Honest by design

Guidance you can check, from an app that knows its limits.

It tells you how sure it is

Every score carries a confidence level and its reasons. A missing night or a skipped log lowers the confidence, never your score.

It learns you first

The first one to two weeks are calibration. daywoven watches your real rhythm before it starts sounding certain, and gets sharper every week.

Your data stays yours

No ads, no analytics, and your data is never sold. Export it or delete your account from inside the app, any time.
