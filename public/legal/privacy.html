Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

Effective November 10, 2026 · Last updated November 10, 2026

daywoven records what you eat, how you move, how you sleep and how you feel, and turns it into guidance for your day. That is health information about your body, and it is the most personal category of data there is. This policy says exactly what we hold, why we hold it, who else can see it, and how you get it back or get rid of it.

The short version

  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and never have.
  • We use no advertising trackers, ad pixels or behavioral analytics. When subscriptions are enabled, Apple or our website billing processor and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement events as described below.
  • We use no advertising cookies on Daywoven pages. Our external RevenueCat/Stripe checkout may use necessary payment cookies. See the Cookies & Local Storage page for the short list of things we do store on your device.
  • Your health entries are visible to you. They are not visible to other users, and there is no social or sharing feature.
  • You can delete your account from Settings and request a copy of your account information through the privacy contact below. Some records are stored only on your device.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No precise location. The app never asks for GPS. Time zone is all it needs to know what “today” means.
  • No contacts, no calendar, no microphone. Audio recording is switched off in the camera configuration.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID) and no device fingerprinting.
  • No browsing history and no data about your activity in other apps or on other websites.
  • No full date of birth. Your birth year is enough to age-adjust a heart-rate range, so that is all we ask for.
  • No medication list. We deliberately leave this out. Your prescriptions are between you and your doctor, and our guidance is not the kind that should be steering around them.

3. Why we collect it

Each category is collected for a specific job, and for no other job:

  • To run your account — the account category. Without an email address there is nothing to log in to.
  • To give you the product you came for — the health entries, profile, scores and wearable metrics. A readiness score is a calculation over your own history; there is no version of this app that works without it.
  • To keep the app working and safe — technical logs, for diagnosing faults and stopping abuse.
  • To prove we asked you properly — consent records. We are required to be able to show what you agreed to.
  • To bill you, if you subscribe — purchase records.

We do not use your health information for advertising, for training AI models of our own, or to build profiles for anyone else. If that ever changes, we will ask you first and it will be a fresh opt-in, not a policy update you have to notice.

4. Camera, photos and the food scanner

There are three separate things here and they behave differently.

  • Barcode scanning. The camera reads a barcode on your device. The number is then sent to our server and on to food databases to look up the product. The camera image never leaves your device.
  • Meal photo estimation. If you take or choose a photo to estimate a meal, that photo is sent to Anthropic’s API so a model can identify the foods in it. The photo is used for that single estimate. daywoven does not store your meal photos. What we keep is the resulting text estimate — “about 140 g chicken thigh” — and a note of which model produced it.
  • Food search. The words you type go to our search endpoint and on to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central. Search requests are sent without any account identifier, so those lookups are not tied to you by us.

Camera and photo-library access are requested at the moment you first use a feature that needs them, with a plain explanation, and you can refuse and still use the app: every food can be entered by search or by typing the barcode.

5. Where AI is used

When the optional meal-photo feature is enabled, daywoven sends the photo you choose to Anthropic to estimate foods and portions. You trigger each request. The current app does not provide an AI chat service; its readiness and guidance calculations use deterministic rules.

  • We send the minimum context the task needs — your chosen meal photo and optional meal note — rather than your full history.
  • For meal photo estimates, we ask for your separate, explicit permission for each photo request before opening the camera or photo picker and sending the selected photo to Anthropic through our server. The checkbox starts unchecked each time you open the photo entry screen. You can cancel, leave it unchecked, or use food search instead. This permission is not saved as a standing opt-in in Settings.
  • Anthropic processes these requests on our behalf as a service provider. Under their commercial API terms, inputs are not used to train their models.
  • An AI answer is not a medical opinion. It is a guess from a language model, it can be confidently wrong, and it is labelled as an estimate in the app for that reason.

8. Your choices and rights

These are available to everyone who uses daywoven, not only to people in places whose laws demand them. You do not need to cite a statute at us.

  • See it — ask what we hold about you.
  • Export it — request a machine-readable copy of the account information we hold by contacting the privacy address below. Device-only records are not included in a server export.
  • Correct it — fix anything wrong, mostly by editing it in the app.
  • Delete it — use Settings → Delete account, enter your current password, and type DELETE to confirm. This removes the account and its account-owned database records. Daywoven also attempts to clear this account’s local records on this device and reports a cleanup failure. Other devices may retain local records until you remove their app data. Original Apple Health records are not deleted. This cannot be undone.
  • Delete part of it — edit or remove individual entries where the app provides those controls, or contact us about the records you want removed.
  • Withdraw a consent — decline photo-AI permission or revoke camera, photo, or Apple Health permissions in your device settings. Manual entry remains available.
  • Object, or ask us to restrict processing — tell us and we will deal with it rather than sending you a form.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of the above. There is no worse version of daywoven for people who ask.

Write to admin@daywovenapp.com and we will respond within 45 days, extending once if a request is genuinely complicated and telling you if we do. We will need to be reasonably sure it is you asking, which normally means the request coming from your account’s email address. An authorised agent can act for you with written permission.

If we get it wrong, you can complain to us first, and then to the California Privacy Protection Agency, the California Attorney General, or your own state’s regulator.

9. California residents

We are a California business and most of this policy is written to California standards already. Specifically:

  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising, which is the specific thing the California Consumer Privacy Act means by that word. We have never done either. There is therefore nothing for a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link to switch off — but if that ever changes, the link will appear before the practice starts.
  • Sensitive personal information. Your health entries are sensitive personal information. We use them only to deliver the service you asked for, which is one of the permitted purposes, and not to infer characteristics about you for anyone else’s benefit. You may direct us to limit their use, though in practice limiting them means turning the product off.
  • The categories we collect, our purposes, the third parties we disclose to, and our retention periods are in sections 2, 3, 6 and 7 above. In the twelve months before the date at the top of this page we disclosed personal information only to the service providers in section 6.
  • Your rights to know, delete, correct, and to data portability, plus the right not to be retaliated against, are in section 8.

We are not a healthcare provider, and HIPAA does not apply to us. daywoven is not a doctor, a clinic, an insurer or a billing service, so the federal health-privacy rules that cover those do not cover us — and we will never claim to be “HIPAA compliant”, because for a company like ours that claim would be meaningless. What does apply to us is California’s own medical-information law, the general consumer-protection rules, and the promises on this page, which we intend to keep.

10. Washington, Nevada and other health-privacy states

Washington’s My Health My Data Act, Nevada’s SB 370 and similar laws give consumer health data its own separate protections. Rather than run two standards, we apply the strictest one to everybody:

  • We collect consumer health data only with your opt-in consent, gathered for a stated purpose at the point of collection.
  • We do not sell consumer health data, to anyone, for anything. No authorisation form exists because there is nothing to authorise.
  • Sharing with the service providers in section 6 happens only to the extent needed to deliver a feature you have asked for.
  • You can withdraw consent and request deletion of consumer health data at any time, at admin@daywovenapp.com, and we will pass deletion requests on to the providers that hold it.

11. Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS. Nothing is sent in the clear.
  • Your database rows are protected by row-level security, so a query for someone else’s data returns nothing rather than trusting the app to behave.
  • On a phone, your login token is held in the operating system’s secure keystore — Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed by our authentication provider. We cannot read yours.
  • Access to production data is limited to people who need it to run the service.

No system is perfect, and anyone who tells you otherwise is selling something. If a breach affects your information we will notify you and the relevant regulators as the law requires — and because we are a health app, that includes the Federal Trade Commission under the Health Breach Notification Rule. To report a vulnerability, write to admin@daywovenapp.com; we will not pursue good-faith researchers.

12. Age limit

daywoven is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we do not direct the app at children.

The reason is not paperwork. An app that evaluates food, logs nutrition and suggests movement is not something we are prepared to put in front of a developing body or a teenager at risk of an eating disorder without supervision we cannot provide. If you believe a minor has created an account, tell us at admin@daywovenapp.com and we will delete it.

13. Where we operate

daywoven is operated from the United States and your information is stored and processed there, in the AWS US East (Ohio, us-east-2) region. United States law does not offer the same protections as some other countries.

daywoven is currently offered in the United States only and is not directed at people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. If we open there later we will publish the additional information those laws require, including a lawful basis for processing health data and a transfer mechanism, before doing so.

14. Do Not Track

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. There is still no agreed standard for what a site must do about it, so we do not respond to it specifically — but the reason it does not matter here is that we do not track you across other sites or services in the first place, whatever your browser says. We also do not allow anyone else to.
