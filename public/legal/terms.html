3. What the numbers in the app actually are

3.1 Scores and estimates

Readiness scores, food grades, sleep debt, circadian estimates, calorie figures and every other number in Healthi are estimates produced by our own models from the data available. They are opinions expressed as numbers. They are not measurements, they are not clinically validated, and they will sometimes be wrong — particularly when there is little data to work from, which is why the app shows you its confidence and its reasoning. A calorie figure derived from a photograph is a guess about a picture.

Food quality grades reflect our assessment against our published methodology. Reasonable nutritionists disagree about how to weigh these things, and a low grade is our view of a product’s composition, not a statement that it is unsafe or that its manufacturer has done anything wrong.

3.2 Allergens — read this one

Never rely on Healthi to tell you whether a food is safe for an allergy or intolerance.

Product information comes from third-party databases and from what manufacturers publish. It can be incomplete, out of date, transcribed wrongly, or missing entirely. A recipe can change while a database record does not. An absent allergen warning in Healthi means we have no information — it never means the food is free of that allergen, and the app is built deliberately never to claim a food is “free from” anything.

Always read the physical package label, and follow your doctor’s or allergist’s advice. If you have a severe allergy, this app is not part of your safety plan.
