Food and product data

Open Food Facts

Packaged food records, ingredient lists and product photographs come from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database built by volunteers.

  • Database: licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0.
  • Individual contents: licensed under the Database Contents License (DbCL) v1.0.
  • Product photographs: licensed by their contributors under CC BY-SA 3.0. Where daywoven shows a product photo, it is served from Open Food Facts and remains the work of the contributor who took it.

Open Food Facts records are contributed by the public and are not verified by us. They can be incomplete or out of date — which is why daywoven tells you where a number came from, and why it never tells you a food is free of an allergen. See Terms § 3.2.

USDA FoodData Central

Food composition data from FoodData Central, U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. As a work of the United States government it is in the public domain. The USDA does not endorse daywoven, and nothing here should be read as suggesting it does.

U.S. Food and Drug Administration

Regulatory reference data, including Daily Values and labelling rules, from FDA publications and the openFDA API. Public domain. The FDA does not endorse daywoven, and daywoven is not FDA-approved, FDA-cleared or FDA-registered.

Published research

Where the app cites scientific literature, the citation identifies the source and the summary is written in our own words. We do not reproduce copyrighted article text.

Corrections

If you own rights in something shown in daywoven and believe it is being used incorrectly, or if a product record is wrong, write to admin@daywoven.com and we will look into it promptly. Product data errors are often best fixed at the source on Open Food Facts, where they will reach every app using the database, and we will help you do that.
