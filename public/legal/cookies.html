Cookies & Local Storage

Version 1.0 · Effective November 10, 2026

daywoven does not use cookies. Not necessary ones, not analytics ones, not advertising ones — none. This page exists because “we don’t use cookies” is a claim worth being able to check, so below is the complete list of what the app does keep on your device, and what each item is for.

Why there is no cookie banner

A consent banner exists to get your permission for storage that is not essential — analytics, advertising, profiling. daywoven has none of that to ask about. Everything in the table below is either required to keep you logged in or is a preference you set yourself, both of which are exempt from consent requirements under the ePrivacy rules and are not “sales” or “sharing” under California law.

Putting a banner up anyway would imply there is hidden tracking to switch off. There isn’t, and we would rather show you the list.

If that ever changes — if we add analytics or any third-party tag — we will ask for your consent before it loads, not after, and this page will be updated first.

What daywoven stores on your device

On the web the app uses your browser’s localStorage. On a phone it uses the operating system’s app storage, and your login token specifically goes into the secure keystore (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android) rather than ordinary storage.

Every item daywoven writes to your device
NamePurposeTypeLifetime
sb-…-auth-token Keeps you logged in, so you are not asked for your password on every screen. Set by our authentication provider. Strictly necessary Until you log out, or the session expires
healthi:onboarding:* Remembers that you finished setup, so you are not walked through it twice. Strictly necessary Until you log out or reset
healthi:movement:routines:v1 Your saved workout routines, held on the device so they open instantly. Preference you set Until you delete them

healthi:demo:* Only in the public demo: keeps demo entries on your device so the demo has something to show. Nothing here reaches our servers. Strictly necessary (demo only) Until you reset the demo

What is not there

  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok pixel, Google Ads or any other advertising or measurement tag.
  • No session-recording or heatmap tools.
  • No third-party cookies, and no embedded content that could set them — no YouTube players, no Google Fonts loaded at runtime, no social widgets, no chat bubbles, no embedded maps. Our fonts are bundled with the app rather than fetched from anyone else.
  • No advertising identifier, no fingerprinting, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Our hosting provider

Our web hosting sits behind Cloudflare. Cloudflare may set a security cookie of its own, such as __cf_bm, to tell humans from bots. That is a security function we do not control the contents of and cannot read; it carries no advertising purpose. See Cloudflare’s cookie documentation if you want the detail.

How to clear it

  • In the app: logging out clears your session. Settings has a reset for demo data.
  • On the web: clearing site data for this site in your browser removes everything in the table. The app will then behave as if you are new, which is the point.
  • Deleting your account removes what is on our servers as well. See the Privacy Policy.

Clearing this storage does not lose your health history if you have an account, because that lives on the server. It does lose anything listed above as device-only, including all demo data.
