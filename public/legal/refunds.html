Refund Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 10, 2026

daywoven is free to download. When subscriptions are enabled, you may purchase Premium through the daywoven website or, where available, using Apple's In-App Purchase system. The billing provider shown on your receipt determines how to cancel or request a refund.

How to cancel a subscription

Website billing: Open the RevenueCat Billing customer portal using the link in your subscription confirmation or renewal emails. You can cancel future renewals there. If you cannot find that link, contact support with your payment receipt.

Apple billing: Open your Apple Account subscription settings and cancel the daywoven subscription there. Cancellation usually stops future renewals and does not automatically refund the current billing period.

How to request a refund

Website purchases: Contact admin@daywoven.com with the subscription invoice and a description of your request. We review eligibility under applicable consumer law and the terms disclosed at checkout; we can process eligible RevenueCat Billing refunds through our billing provider. No unconditional refund period is promised here.

Apple purchases: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund. Apple determines eligibility and processes Apple-billed refunds, which we cannot reverse ourselves.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be waived.

Contact

Questions about access or billing can be sent to admin@daywoven.com.
