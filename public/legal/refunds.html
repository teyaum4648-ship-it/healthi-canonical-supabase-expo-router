Refund Policy

If Healthi is not for you, we would rather refund you than keep your money. The one thing that complicates it is where you bought it, because if you paid Apple or Google then they hold the money and their rules decide, not ours.

First, work out who you paid

Where to go for a refund

Inside the Android app Google. Use Google Play subscriptions. Google’s policy applies.
Directly from us on the web Us. Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. The policy below is ours and we control it.

Why we cannot just refund an app-store purchase. When you buy through Apple or Google, they are the merchant of record. The money goes to them, and only they can return it. We do not get to promise you a refund they might refuse — so we will not pretend otherwise. What we can do is help: if Apple or Google turn you down and you think that is unfair, write to us anyway and we will see what we can do, including credit or an extension.

Our policy, for subscriptions bought directly from us

14-day refund, no reasons needed

Ask within 14 days of your first payment on a new subscription and we will refund it in full. You do not have to explain, and we will not ask you to complete a survey first.

After 14 days

We do not refund the remainder of a period you are part-way through, and monthly subscriptions are not refunded after 14 days. You can cancel at any time, which stops the next charge and leaves your access running until the period you paid for ends.

Annual plans

Beyond the 14-day window, if you cancel an annual plan we will refund the unused whole months, minus any discount you received for paying annually, on request. We are not required to do this; we do it because charging someone for eleven months they will not use is not a business we want.

When we will refund you regardless of the date

  • We charged you by mistake — double billing, a charge after you cancelled, the wrong plan. Always refunded in full, and tell us so we can fix the cause.
  • A paid feature was broken or unavailable for a meaningful stretch and we could not fix it.
  • You were charged after a free trial that you reasonably believed you had cancelled.
  • We materially changed or removed something you were paying for and you do not want to continue.
  • We raised the price and the notice did not reach you before the charge.
  • Fraud — somebody used your card without permission. Contact us and your bank.

When we will not

  • You forgot to cancel and want several past months back. We will refund the most recent charge if you ask promptly; we will not unwind a year.
  • You did not like your results. Healthi gives guidance, not outcomes, and it cannot promise you anything about your body.
  • Your account was closed for a serious breach of the acceptable use rules.
  • Repeat refund-and-resubscribe cycles on the same account.

How to ask

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from your account’s email address with the word “Refund” in the subject. Tell us which plan and roughly when you subscribed. We will reply within 5 business days and, once approved, the refund goes back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days — the last part is your bank’s timing, not ours.

Cancelling is not the same as a refund

Cancelling stops future charges. It does not return money already taken, and it does not cut your access off early — you keep the app until the period you paid for runs out. If you want both, cancel and then ask for a refund.

Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy takes away rights you have under California or federal consumer law, including under California’s automatic renewal law. If this policy and the law disagree, the law wins.
