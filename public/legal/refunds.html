Refund Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective November 10, 2026

daywoven is free to download. If an optional premium subscription is offered in the iOS app, it is purchased and billed through Apple using In-App Purchase. We do not currently sell subscriptions directly through our website.

How to cancel a subscription

Open your Apple Account subscription settings on your device and select the daywoven subscription, if you have one. Cancellation normally stops future renewals; the paid access period ordinarily runs until its expiration date, subject to Apple's terms.

How to request a refund

If Apple billed your subscription, visit reportaproblem.apple.com, select the purchase and follow Apple's refund-request process. Apple determines eligibility and handles refunds for purchases it processes. Our support team can help you locate the correct process but cannot guarantee approval or reverse an Apple charge directly.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be waived.

Contact

Questions about access or billing can be sent to admin@daywoven.com.
